scene.spz is the supplied single-frame Gaussian reconstruction. cassette.glb is
the supplied generated cassette approximation, not a verified original game
model. No reconstruction or image generation is performed by this importer.
The readable viewer starts ambient rotation enabled, permits its pause control
independently of system reduced motion, and supplies the camera/cassette relative
position to navigation. The readable navigation keeps the original broad idle
orbit at every viewing distance, including near the reel. Its first direction
is chosen from the current camera position once after direct input. The intro
approach follows the same progress/track/damping as wheel input, without
an additional idle orbit until the approach has finished. Explicit
source adaptations are recorded in the importer; no opaque compiled bundle is
patched. Manual orbit, zoom damping, track, model transform and playback bridge
remain original. Reduced motion still shortens reel playback and CVA decoration.
motion-track.js and the original THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt are copied unchanged.
Only viewer.js, navigation.js, motion-track.js, the original notices and this
attribution are imported locally. No supplied source is modified.
